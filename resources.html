Student access & licensing

Free & Student Software Access

Many scientific software packages require commercial licenses, which may not always be affordable or readily accessible to students and early-career researchers.

Before purchasing software, students are encouraged to explore free academic licenses, institutional subscriptions, educational discounts, trial versions, and open-source alternatives.

Universities often provide access to specialized chemistry software through institutional licensing arrangements. Students should consult their university IT department, library, or research supervisor about available options.

For freely accessible alternatives, consider Avogadro, Multiwfn, ORCA (subject to its license terms), MOPAC, gnuplot, Zotero, LibreOffice, and Inkscape.

Note: If an official download website is inaccessible in your region, using a VPN may help where permitted, although access is not guaranteed. Always obtain software from trusted sources and comply with applicable licensing conditions.
