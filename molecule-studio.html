Which files will make these structures interactive?

For each published compound, the preferred source is its original optimized .xyz, .mol2, .sdf, Gaussian .log or .fchk file. Supporting-information PDFs or ZIP archives containing Cartesian coordinates also work. Each file should be linked unambiguously to the compound code in its published article. Molecular orbitals require formatted checkpoints or verified cube files in a later phase.

Provenance policy: Graphical abstracts are author-supplied, but do not establish 3D atom positions. We do not invent geometries or show molecules from unpublished work as published research.
